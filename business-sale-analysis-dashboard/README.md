# Sales Analysis Dashboard for KAT Manufacturing

## Overview

Developed an interactive Tableau dashboard to analyze sales performance across customer segments, product lines, geographic regions, and time periods. The dashboard was designed to provide executives and sales managers with a consolidated view of revenue trends and key business drivers.

## Business Problem

KAT Manufacturing needed a centralized reporting solution to monitor sales performance, identify top revenue-generating customers and products, and understand revenue distribution across divisions and customer categories.

## Tools Used

* Tableau
* Microsoft Excel
* Data Visualization
* Business Analytics

## Dashboard Features

* Revenue analysis by customer category
* Quarterly and yearly sales trend analysis
* Top customer identification and ranking
* Geographic sales visualization by division
* Product line performance analysis
* Interactive filtering and drill-down capabilities

## Key Insights

* Materials Processing generated the highest revenue among customer categories.
* A small number of customers contributed a significant portion of total sales.
* Q4 represented the strongest sales quarter in the analyzed period.
* Revenue was concentrated within a few core product lines, particularly Low Yb and Pulsed Yb laser products.
* Sales activity was distributed across multiple international markets including the United States, Germany, Russia, Korea, and the United Kingdom.

## Skills Demonstrated
* Dashboard Development
* Data Visualization
* Business Intelligence
* KPI Analysis
* Data Storytelling

## Outcome

The dashboard enabled stakeholders to quickly identify revenue trends, high-value customers, top-performing products, and geographic sales distribution, supporting data-driven decision-making and strategic sales planning.
